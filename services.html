Questions people ask

How long does a project take?

Most projects take 1 to 6 weeks. Larger ERP work is split into phases, and I confirm the timeline after the first call.

How do you price the work?

A fixed price per project once the scope is agreed. For ongoing work, a monthly block of hours. I ask for 40% upfront, with the rest tied to milestones.

Can you work with an existing system?

Yes. I review the current setup first, then write down what I would change before changing it. If it is not something I can work with safely, I will say so.

Who owns the code and the accounts?

You do. Repositories, domains, and deployment accounts stay in your name, and I work with your accounts where possible.

Are you available full time?

No. I work alongside a full-time engineering role, but I keep a fixed weekly block for freelance work and agree the timeline before we start.

What do you need to start?

A short description of the workflow, access to any existing system, and one person who can approve the scope. A call is usually enough to decide whether we should work together.
